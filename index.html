Hi, I'm Adi. I just like to build stuff.

I'm studying Mechanical Engineering at Georgia Tech. I have seven years of competitive robotics behind me. In high school I competed in both FRC and VEX in a highly competitive region, and I was part of the strategy, design, build, and manufacturing process on both.

It taught me that failing is fine, but time is money, and if you don't design with intent, you fall behind. Rapid prototyping is great for killing bad ideas, but it's hard to commit to one path if you don't do it properly.

I also see engineering as a form of expression, so I like my robots to be interesting and good to look at.

Blaze (V3)

VEX High Stakes Pneumatics CNC polycarbonate

Ranked top 5 in Southern California

Blaze is the third version of our VEX High Stakes robot: a fast six-motor drive, a touch-it-own-it intake, a pneumatic mobile goal clamp, and a one-motor wall stake arm.

It made us tournament finalists at Supernova Spectacular. Ranked 6th at the 2025 California Region 4 State Championship (6-2 in qualifications) and competed as the 4th-seeded alliance.

Breakthrough 2.0 (V6)

VEX Over Under Pneumatics Custom plastics

Ranked top 5 in Southern California

V6 was built to fix what went wrong at our last tournament: slow scoring, a bent drivetrain, an unreliable hang, and a heavy robot.
